A note before you decide anything

When you found this page, it was probably late, and you probably felt like you were failing. I've sat across from hundreds of sons and daughters carrying the same quiet fear: that moving a parent into a home means giving up on them.

It doesn't. You are not leaving your mother behind. You are handing her day to people who can give her more than one exhausted person can alone — a doctor down the hall, a warm kitchen, someone to talk to at three in the afternoon.

The guilt is real, and it means you love her. But love isn't the same as doing it all by yourself. So come and see us — not for a tour, for lunch. Watch how we talk to the people who live here, and decide with your own eyes. If it isn't right, you'll know, and that's fine too.
